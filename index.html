PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

Holding a Master's in Statistics from the University of Dhaka, I have developed expertise in research design, survey tools, qualitative guideline development, data collection, analysis, and reporting. With hands-on experience in over 25 mixed-method research studies, I bring a strong track record in generating high-quality evidence to inform program design, drive implementation excellence, and shape policy decisions.

Beyond research, I actively contribute to organizational priorities including CSP, DEI, GESI, humanitarian response, and staff well-being through cultural and sports engagement—demonstrating both analytical depth and a people-centered approach at SCI. His previous appointment before joining at SCI was as a Senior Statistician at BRAC James P Grant School of Public Health. I also worked as a Senior Teaching Fellow for the Biostatistics course of the MPH program. Previously, he also worked in the a2i program of the Prime Minister's office in Bangladesh. He has bachelors and master's degrees in Statistics, Biostatistics, and Informatics from the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh.

His work interests include climate change, urban and rural areas, marginalized communities such as brothel and Dalit groups, sexual and reproductive health and rights, children's and adolescents' health, index generation, and the application of behavioral science for service simplification.
